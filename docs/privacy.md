# Tremolo privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Tremolo is an app for Android phones and Wear OS watches that measures arm and hand tremor.
This policy covers both the phone app and the watch app.

## The short version

Tremolo collects nothing. Everything you record stays on your own phone and watch. There is no
account, no server, no advertising and no analytics, and the app has no permission to use the
internet.

## What the app stores on your devices

- **Movement recordings.** Short recordings from the watch's gyroscope and accelerometer, and
  the tremor figures calculated from them.
- **What you enter.** DBS settings and their names, photos you add of a programmer screen,
  daily ratings, the kind of day, logged events, medication names and reminder times. When
  you ask the app to read the values from such a photo, the text is recognised on the phone
  itself; the photo is not sent anywhere.
- **Test results.** Scores from the tapping and spiral tests. The speech test analyses sound
  from the microphone while it runs and keeps only the resulting scores, not the audio.
- **Sleep times.** If you allow it, Tremolo reads sleep times from Health Connect to leave out
  measurements taken while you were asleep. It reads them when needed and does not copy them
  elsewhere. It never writes to Health Connect.

This is health information. It is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, where
other apps cannot read it.

## What leaves your devices

Nothing, unless you send it yourself. Data leaves your phone only when you:

- share a report (PDF) or an export (CSV), or
- save a backup file,

and then it goes only to the app or place you choose. The watch sends its recordings to your
own phone over the connection between the two, and to nowhere else.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Sensors on the watch, at a high sampling rate | To record wrist movement |
| Running in the background on the watch | To measure at random moments during a trial |
| Alarms on the watch | To take the planned checks on time |
| Notifications | Reminders, and a warning when the watch stops measuring |
| Microphone on the phone | Only during the speech test, when you start it |
| Sleep from Health Connect | Optional, to leave out measurements taken while asleep |
| Camera or photo picker | Only when you add a photo to a DBS setting |

## Deleting your data

You can delete a finished trial from its report. Uninstalling the app removes everything it
stored. Backup files and reports you saved or shared are yours to delete where you put them.

## Children

Tremolo is not directed at children.

## Not a medical device

Tremolo is not a medical device and its method has not been clinically validated. Change
stimulation only as your clinician allows.
